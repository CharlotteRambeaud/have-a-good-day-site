Last updated: 12 November 2026

What the app stores

Your sign-in identifier. On iPhone, the app uses Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an identifier specific to this app, and if you hide your email address, we only ever see the relay address Apple provides. On Android, it uses Google sign-in: Google gives us your email address and an identifier.

The first name or nickname you enter. It is shown only to the person you are connected with.

Your drawing for the day. It is sent to your person. At 4 a.m. in their time zone the drawing expires: within the following hour, its image and any words written on it are erased from the server. The server keeps no history of your drawings.

The photos you choose for the days without a drawing, up to ten. Only your person can see them, until you remove them.

Stickers cut out of your photos stay on your phone. They only leave it when you place them in a drawing you send.

A notification token from Apple (iPhone) or Google Firebase (Android), used to silently wake your person's app so their widget updates. No visible notification is ever sent.

Your time zone and language, to place the 4 a.m. boundary and show the right language in the widget.

Usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and make it better, the server records, linked to your account:

  • the days you open the app, your platform (iPhone or Android), the app version and your last visit;
  • for each drawing you send: its date, the names of the stamps you placed (such as "heart" or "wine glass"), and whether it contains a photo or text — never the image, the photo or the words;
  • the dates you connect with someone, or unpair.

These statistics stay on our server: they are not shared or sent to any analytics service. When you delete your account, they are no longer linked to any account.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics.
  • No data sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, location or microphone. The camera and photo library are only used for the images you choose yourself.

Where your data lives

Data is hosted with Supabase, which provides the database and image storage. On Android, Google Firebase carries the messages that wake the app; they contain no drawing. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Only the two people in a couple can read that couple's drawings and photos; this boundary is enforced by the server, not just by the app.

Your rights

Delete a drawing: wait for the night, or replace it.

End the connection: Settings → Unpair. Shared drawings are deleted for both of you.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. The account, the connection and the shared images are permanently erased. Deleting the app is not enough: the account lives on the server.
