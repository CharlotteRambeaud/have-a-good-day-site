Last updated: 7 September 2026

What the app stores

Your sign-in identifier. The app only accepts Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier specific to this app; it cannot be used to identify you anywhere else. If you choose to hide your email address, we only ever see the relay address Apple provides.

The first name or nickname you enter. It is shown only to the person you are connected with.

Your drawing for the day. It is sent to your partner and deleted automatically every night, at 4 a.m. in your time zone. Drawings are never archived: the app keeps no history.

The photo you choose for the days you haven't drawn. It is visible to your partner only, and replaced as soon as you pick another one.

A notification token from Apple, used to silently wake your partner's app so their widget updates. No visible notification is ever sent.

Your time zone and language, to place the 4 a.m. boundary and show the right language in the widget.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics.
  • No data sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or to your photo library beyond the single image you select yourself.

Where your data lives

Data is hosted with Supabase, which provides the database and image storage. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Only the two people in a couple can read that couple's drawings and photos; this boundary is enforced by the server, not just by the app.

Your rights

Delete a drawing: wait for the night, or replace it.

End the connection: Settings → Unpair. Shared drawings are deleted for both of you.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. The account, the connection and the shared images are permanently erased. Deleting the app is not enough: the account lives on the server.

For any question about your data: contact.haveagooday@gmail.com
